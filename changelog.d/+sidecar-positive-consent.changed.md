Require `AGENTCOOKIE=on` or affirmative `BROWSER_CONSENT` before reading X cookies through the agentcookie sidecar. Eligible Linux, Mac mini, and Darwin sink hosts no longer enable sidecar reads from binary presence alone. Explicit refusals still win, and direct `AUTH_TOKEN`+`CT0` credentials remain usable. Sidecar-only users can set `AGENTCOOKIE=on` with `BROWSER_CONSENT` and `FROM_BROWSER` absent. Doctor shows this opt-in hint for an installed, unadmitted sidecar and refreshes cached advice when `AGENTCOOKIE` changes.
