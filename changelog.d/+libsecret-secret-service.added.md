Secret Service (libsecret) credential source: keys stored in a Linux/BSD desktop keyring under the attribute `service=last30days-<KEY>` are now picked up automatically, the desktop analog of the existing macOS Keychain and `pass`(1) sources. Store them with the new `scripts/setup-keyring.sh` (mirrors `setup-keychain.sh`, plus `--collection` for a password-protected keyring), and point the loader at a different namespace with `LAST30DAYS_KEYRING_PREFIX`.
