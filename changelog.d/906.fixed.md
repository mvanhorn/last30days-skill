Reddit enrichment slots are now awarded to the busiest threads instead of the highest-upvote ones: within each entity tier, posts with a known comment count sort by that count descending, so a lower-upvote thread with hundreds of comments gets its comments fetched before a viral post that has few. RSS-only posts, where a zero comment count means "unknown" rather than "no comments", keep their existing score-first ordering and are never pushed to the back of the tier.
