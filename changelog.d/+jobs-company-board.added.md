Two host flags for the jobs lane. `--company "Name"` (at most two, comma-separated) names the company behind a person or product topic, listed or not, so a founder of a private company gets that company's board (Palmer Luckey: `--company "Anduril"`). `--jobs-board <URL>` passes the company's job-board URL the host found by web search (Greenhouse, Ashby, Lever, Workable, SmartRecruiters, Workday); the engine reads that board first and gives a digest only when the board names the company or the official site links to it, otherwise the footer count alone with the reason on stderr. A URL on an unsupported system is ignored with one stderr line.
