Self-hosted transcription: `LAST30DAYS_WHISPER_URL` points caption-free transcription at any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (whisper.cpp's `whisper-server`, LocalAI, vLLM). It is tried before Groq/OpenAI, needs no key, and keeps audio on the machine; the provider chain still falls through to the hosted backstops when the local server is down. `LAST30DAYS_WHISPER_MODEL` and `LAST30DAYS_WHISPER_KEY` cover servers with a pinned model name or a gateway token.
