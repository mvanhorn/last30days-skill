Reddit keyless RSS discovery is now resilient to anti-bot 403/429 responses: requests are serialized per host with bounded Retry-After cooldowns, subreddit feeds fall back to old.reddit.com after a blocked or malformed www response, and successful feeds are cached briefly to bridge a bounded outage. YouTube's anti-bot/sign-in interstitial is detected in yt-dlp stderr and the watch page so it is no longer misreported as zero demand or missing captions.
