**Next30Days: every brief ends with predictions about what happens next.** Every ordinary `/last30days <topic>` run now gathers the topic's open questions (live prediction markets with odds, close date, and a link to the specific market, plus scheduled events, pending decisions, and expected releases with dates and links) and closes with a `Next 30 days:` section holding one or two predictions. Each has a probability, the baseline it is measured against (the market price, or the status-quo outcome), a resolve date, and a one-line reason. The engine refuses a prediction that only restates the baseline, and a market with no evidence to depart from is shown as the item to watch instead. Predictions are logged to a local append-only ledger in the save directory (nothing is logged when saving is disabled, and `LAST30DAYS_NEXT30DAYS=off` turns them off). A later run on the same topic first shows how its earlier calls turned out, market-anchored calls settle automatically, and `/last30days scorecard` leads with the calls that departed from a market baseline and their skill against the market's price. New `--open-questions` and `--forecast-file` flags and `forecast add|list|due|settle|grade|scorecard` commands back it.
