**Next30Days: forecasts that grade themselves.** Ask what happens next with a topic and, after the usual research, the skill logs 3-5 dated, falsifiable predictions with probabilities and evidence to a local append-only ledger. Calls anchored to a Polymarket market settle automatically when the market resolves; the rest are graded with an evidence link once their date arrives. `/last30days scorecard` reports hit rate, Brier score, calibration, and how the same calls scored against the market price. New `forecast add|list|due|settle|grade|scorecard` engine commands and a `--forecast-file` flag back it.
