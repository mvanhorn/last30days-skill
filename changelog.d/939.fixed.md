The LinkedIn source now honors the requested date window instead of always querying a hardcoded `last-month` bucket, walks the response cursor instead of stopping after the first ~10 posts, and shortens over-length queries that the endpoint rejects with a 404. Because results inside a bucket are relevance-ranked rather than recency-ranked, the search queries both the narrow and the covering bucket and unions them. A bucket that fails after another succeeded now returns `partial: True` with the first error rather than reading as a complete, low-volume result.
