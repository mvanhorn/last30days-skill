New `nfl_polymarket` source and a `Market says` report header. For any resolved NFL team, player, game, or league topic it pulls Polymarket game moneylines (with the main spread and total), season futures (champion, conference, division, playoffs, win total) with 7-day changes, award markets, and coaching/roster markets, matched structurally rather than by keyword. Keyless. `--search odds` selects it.
