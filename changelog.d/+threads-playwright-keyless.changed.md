Threads now searches without a ScrapeCreators key: a headless browser (Playwright, run through `uv run --with playwright` when the interpreter lacks it) reads the public, logged-out threads.com search page, both the fresh and the top result lists. It stays opt-in (`INCLUDE_SOURCES=threads` or `--search threads`); `LAST30DAYS_THREADS_BROWSER=off` turns it off and `LAST30DAYS_THREADS_PYTHON` picks the interpreter. Queries are capped at three words with shorter fallbacks, since Threads answers "No results." to longer ones.
