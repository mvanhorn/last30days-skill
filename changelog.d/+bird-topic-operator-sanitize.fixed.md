The Bird X lane no longer forwards advanced-search operators from a topic. A topic containing `from:`, `since:`, `lang:`, leading negation, or a bare `and`/`or` used to reach X as syntax: `since:2020 docker tips` emitted a duplicate `since:` beside the research window, and `from:someone` silently restricted the search to one account. The same operator filter already guards the xurl lane. Queries are also bounded to X's 512-character limit, which the lane previously exceeded on long topics; when the bound trims a quoted topic it degrades to bare keywords rather than leave an unterminated phrase.
