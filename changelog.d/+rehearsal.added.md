**Rehearsal: forecast the reception of your own draft.** Paste a launch post, tweet, landing page, or pitch and ask how it will land. The skill researches how the same communities reacted to two or three comparable launches in the last 30 days, then predicts the reception venue by venue, with every predicted reaction backed by a real quoted comment. The draft's text and any unannounced name are never placed in a search query, and the predictions can be logged to the forecast ledger and graded after launch.
