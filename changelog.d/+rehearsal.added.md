**Rehearsal: forecast the reception of your own draft.** Paste a launch post, tweet, landing page, or pitch and ask how it will land. The skill finds two or three comparable launches from the last 30 days, the engine pulls each venue's top-voted reactions to them (`--reactions`), and the brief predicts the reception venue by venue, with every predicted reaction tied to a real quoted comment. A private file (`--private-file`, or `--nothing-private` when nothing is secret) holds the draft and any names you mark private; the engine refuses the run if any of it would go into an outbound request, and a guarded run writes no cache or brief.
