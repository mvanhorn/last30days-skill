Ranking without an LLM reranker now weighs real engagement: a 380-point Hacker News story or a 254-upvote Reddit thread no longer ranks below 1-3-like posts that happen to be newer. Engagement is scored on an absolute per-platform scale instead of within each search's own results, freshness is counted once, the low-score cliff applies only to LLM-scored results, and first-party posts are lifted only when they are on topic (or the handle is the topic itself). The "All Items" list now shows real scores instead of 0 or 1.
