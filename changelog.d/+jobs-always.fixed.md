The jobs lane no longer depends on the host naming `jobs` in its plan. On a host-planned run about a company (`--official-site`, a usable `--ticker` pair, `--company`, or a topic the engine resolves to a company) the engine adds `jobs` to the primary subquery itself and logs `[Planner] Added jobs to primary: ...`; `EXCLUDE_SOURCES=jobs`, `--quick`, and a `--search` list without `jobs` still leave it out. The lane also finds more boards without help: it follows a careers page's link to its board page (`/careers/board`), and when the company's own slug has no board it tries the name the careers page states for its organization (`Anduril Industries` for `Anduril`), giving a digest only when the board states that same name.
