Patent Secret Signals now appear only for a filing that is out of character for the company or describes a product it has not shipped, announced or had reported. A tie to a story in the report no longer qualifies a filing, filings listed by title only are never signals, and the section is one short paragraph with no counts sentence or closing disclaimer. The patent digest and footer no longer say "confirmed" or "name-matched": a company's own holding, licensing and numbered entities count as the company, and other filers with the company's name are marked "not verified".
