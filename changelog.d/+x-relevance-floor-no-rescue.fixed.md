An all-off-topic X batch is no longer rescued back into the ranking. `prune_low_relevance` ended in `return filtered or items`, so when *every* item in a batch failed the relevance floor the whole batch came back unpruned — on X that meant three crypto-promo posts matching one generic query token survived, and the freshness and engagement terms then floated one of them above the run's genuinely on-topic Reddit thread. Batches made up only of social sources with no relevance gate of their own now prune to empty (visible as `no-results` in `source_status`) instead. Reddit keeps the rescue, since its own floor runs earlier, and pinned handle lanes keep it too — a named account's posts are wanted whether or not they match the topic lexically.
