NFL entity resolution and a curated beat-writer lane. Every topic is resolved to a team, player/coach, game, or the league (`--team KC`, `--player "Patrick Mahomes"`, or automatically from nicknames, abbreviations, cities, and "X vs Y"). A resolved entity seeds `r/nfl` plus the team subreddit and searches a built-in roster of beat writers and national insiders on X at full weight (`--beat-writers off` or `NFL30_BEAT_WRITERS=off` disables; override the roster at `~/.config/nfl30/beat_writers.json`). Beat-writer posts render as `@handle (Outlet)`.
