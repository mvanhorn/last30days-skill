The quote pool is now chosen by position, not like count: each thread, video, reel or story gives its top comment and its #2, all top comments are listed first in story order and then the #2s, each entry is labelled `top comment`, `#2 comment` or `post`, and entries from a result the humor score rated funny lead their story and are tagged `funny`. Quote text is cut at a sentence end instead of mid-sentence, raw URLs and an X reply's leading handles are dropped, and a comment that is itself a quotation no longer prints doubled quote marks. Reports treat six quote lines per story as a ceiling, not a target, and a recommendations run's closing checklist no longer asks for story quote lines. Bot comments, machine markup, a creator's or original poster's own comments, quoted-parent lines and markdown emphasis no longer reach the pool, GitHub comments need a reaction, and a thread joins only when its top community comment has at least 5 votes.
