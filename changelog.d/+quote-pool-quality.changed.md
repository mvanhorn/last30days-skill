The quote pool now holds only quotes that read well and stand up beside their story's best: an entry needs a tenth of the top count on its platform in the same story, text is cut at a sentence end instead of mid-sentence, raw URLs and an X reply's leading handles are dropped, and a comment that is itself a quotation no longer prints doubled quote marks. Reports treat six quote lines per story as a ceiling, not a target, and a recommendations run's closing checklist no longer asks for story quote lines.
