Company topics get a Yahoo Finance footer line: the share-price change over the research window with both closes, the S&P 500 for US listings, and the largest single-day move. It appears when `yahoo-finance-pp-cli` is on PATH and the topic resolves to a listed company or the person who leads one; the same numbers are in the evidence as a Market Data block and add nothing to the item counts. `EXCLUDE_SOURCES=yahoo_finance` turns the line off with no call to Yahoo Finance, and adding `jobs` to that list also skips the Wikidata company lookup. When the Wikidata lookup fails, the topic text is sent to Yahoo Finance's symbol search as a fallback and its first stock is used only on an exact name match with a US symbol.
