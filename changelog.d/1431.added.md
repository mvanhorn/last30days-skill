The jobs lane detects the hiring platform a company's careers page runs on (Oracle Recruiting Cloud, Jibe, Phenom, TalentBrew, Eightfold on any host, or a linked Workday board) and reads its public listing, and reads Google's and IBM's job sites, so a company run now gets a Jobs Digest for Oracle, JPMorgan, Dell, AMD, Adobe, Cisco, Disney, IBM, Salesforce, Intel and Google. A careers site with no readable listing but a job sitemap (SAP, Uber, Walmart) gets a titles-only digest. Set `LAST30DAYS_JOBS_RESPECT_ROBOTS=1` to skip any listing a site's robots.txt disallows.
