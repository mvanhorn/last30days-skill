**Patient Zero: trace where a story started and how it spread.** Asking where a story, meme, or claim started adds a spread timeline: the earliest post found, each platform's first sighting in date order with the range that platform was searched over, posts that link to an earlier one, the peak day, the biggest post, and the limits of the trace. When the earliest post sits at the start of the window, the engine searches again over 90 days, then 365, on sources that can look that far back. The trail names venues, not individual posters. The HTML brief draws it as a timeline, and `--trace-exclude` leaves a rejected post out of a re-render. New `--trace-origin` flag for single-topic compact, Markdown, and HTML runs.
