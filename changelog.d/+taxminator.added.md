New keyless source: **Taxminator**, the Uzbek prediction market (`taxminator.uz`). Adds crowd forecasts on Central Asian football, CIS chess, esports, the Uzbek economy and entertainment — coverage Polymarket does not carry. Topics are matched client-side against all three of a market's titles (uz/ru/en) plus its outcome labels, using the same overlap/similarity rules and relevance floors as Polymarket, so the source stays silent when a topic is off-topic. No API key, no CLI, always on. Because Taxminator is scored in points rather than money, the skill cites it as a crowd share of N predictors and never as money-backed odds; below the crowd-reveal floor the market is still reported with its predictor count and no split.
